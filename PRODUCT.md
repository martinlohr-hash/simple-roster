# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owner/manager of a small business — built as a personal internal tool, not a commercial SaaS product. The admin opens the schedule between other tasks (between a morning rush, before a shift starts) and needs things to work immediately. Employees use the dashboard briefly to clock in and out; their context is a phone or shared screen on the way to a workstation.

## Product Purpose

Shift planning and time tracking for small teams (up to ~10 people, 2 locations). Admin plans the week visually, employees clock in/out, wage costs are calculated automatically and can be exported. Success means the admin spends almost no time on scheduling logistics, and employees never wonder where they need to be or how many hours they've worked.

## Brand Personality

**Three words:** clear, warm, fast.

Friendly and approachable — the feel of a well-made coworker tool, not enterprise software. Linear's speed and density, but with human warmth: not cold, not clinical, not hard-edged. Confident without being stiff. Voice is direct and helpful, never corporate.

## Anti-references

- **When I Work / Deputy** — over-featured SaaS with pricing tiers, feature bloat, upsell banners. SimpleRoster does less and does it better.
- **HR enterprise tools (SAP, Workday)** — gray table soup, no visual hierarchy, depressing to open. The UI should feel like something a person made, not a committee.

## References

- **Linear** (the speed, density, and confidence) — but warmer and more approachable than Linear's cold precision.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed over ceremony.** Every action should take one click fewer than expected. No confirmation dialogs for non-destructive actions. No loading skeletons for fast operations.
2. **Data is the product, UI steps back.** The schedule and numbers are always front and center. Chrome stays out of the way.
3. **Warm clarity.** Human warmth in spacing, color temperature, and copy — but unambiguous hierarchy. Friendly ≠ vague.
4. **Built for daily repeat use.** Interactions must be instant and predictable. Animations serve comprehension, not delight.
5. **No dead ends.** Every page has a clear primary action. Empty states explain what to do next, not just that nothing is there.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Standard keyboard navigation. Reduced motion respected (already implemented globally). No specific high-contrast or screen-reader requirements beyond baseline AA.
